# పక్కింటి వెంకీ మామ — Episode 1: ఒకే వీధి, రెండు బిల్లులు (v1)

Comedy reel for Saanvika Solar Systems, 30.5 s, 1080×1920, 30 fps, synthesized sound effects baked in.

## How this concept was chosen

Five writers each wrote a different comedic angle (horror trailer, neighbour envy, breaking news, bill villain, kid roasts dad). Three independent judges scored them: a Telugu reels creator, a brand-safety reviewer, and a motion-design producer. Totals out of 180:

| Concept | Score |
|---|---|
| Neighbour envy (Venky Mama) | 139 |
| Kid roasts dad | 133 |
| Breaking news | 128 |
| Bill villain | 127 |
| Horror trailer | 114 |

Four recurring characters were generated once as reference portraits, and every shot was generated from those references so the same faces appear in all 12 shots.

## Story (what happens on screen)

1. Subbarao hugs the fridge: "బాబోయ్! బిల్లు ₹3,200*"

7. Subbarao with a stethoscope on the neighbour's meter, googly eyes, meter yawning "zzz"
8. "మామా... ఎలా?!" shouted over the parapet
9. Venky silently points up at the panels: "మీటర్ కాదు... పైకి చూడు!"
10. Lakshmi, deadpan, already on the phone: "ఇక చాలు. నేను కాల్ చేశా." + Saanvika contact card
