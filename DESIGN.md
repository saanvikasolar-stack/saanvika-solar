## Brand

Saanvika Solar Systems is a Kakinada, Andhra Pradesh rooftop solar installer —
a registered PM Surya Ghar (MNRE) vendor serving coastal Andhra homes with
APEPDCL net metering and end-to-end subsidy paperwork. Visual identity stays
true to the existing logo mark: orange sun rising behind a navy house with
blue rooftop panels.

## Direction

Uniform Light. Professional, funky, and clean — local-and-trusted voice,
sunny and precise, without gimmicks. Generous whitespace; bold brand-first
heroes; amber/orange CTAs on navy text; panel sky-blue as a secondary brand
accent for links and tech cues (derived surfaces stay within the approved
warm-light / navy / sun-orange families). Authentic Godavari-coast energy,
not Hyderabad corporate.
